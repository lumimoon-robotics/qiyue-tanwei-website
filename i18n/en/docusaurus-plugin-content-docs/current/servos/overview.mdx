# ⚙️ Servo Products

Smart bus servos based on the Feetech ST3215 platform, covering diverse torque, speed, and precision requirements.

## Product Lines

| Model | Torque | Speed | Precision | Positioning |
|-------|--------|-------|-----------|-------------|
| [C018](./st3215-c018) | 15kg·cm | 0.15s/60° | 0.3° | Standard |
| [C001](./st3215-c001) | 20kg·cm | 0.12s/60° | 0.2° | High Torque |
| [C044](./st3215-c044) | 10kg·cm | 0.10s/60° | 0.2° | High Speed |
| [C046](./st3215-c046) | 18kg·cm | 0.13s/60° | 0.15° | High Precision |
| [SCS0009](./scs0009) | 5kg·cm | 0.08s/60° | 0.1° | Micro High-Speed |

## Common Features

- **Bus Control** — RS485 multi-drop, up to 254 nodes
- **Full Feedback** — Position, velocity, torque, temperature
- **ROS2 Driver** — Standard `joint_state` + PID tuning
- **Wide Voltage** — 6V-12V, compatible with all robot platforms
